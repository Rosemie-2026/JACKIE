My Dream: Become a Medical Assistant

My career goal is to become a Medical Assistant after completing my studies in Office Administration. I aim to develop both administrative and healthcare skills that will help me work effectively in a medical setting. In the future, I hope to provide quality support to patients and contribute positively to the healthcare field.

Never stop learning - pursue further training, certifications, and personal development.
